Deploys InvoiceShelf, a self-hosted invoicing app for estimates, invoices, recurring invoices, payments and expenses, from the official image with nginx, PHP-FPM and the Laravel scheduler in one container. The SQLite database, uploaded files, generated PDFs and the application key live on the storage volume, and marketplace modules on a second volume. After deployment, open the app and finish the installation wizard: keep the prefilled SQLite path, then create the administrator account and company. Outgoing email is configured in the wizard or later under Settings.
