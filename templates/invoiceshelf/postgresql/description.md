Deploys InvoiceShelf, a self-hosted invoicing app for estimates, invoices, recurring invoices, payments and expenses, from the official image together with a PostgreSQL database. Business data lives in PostgreSQL on its own volume, while uploaded files, generated PDFs and the application key stay on the storage volume and marketplace modules on a third. After deployment, open the app and finish the installation wizard: in the database step enter host invoiceshelf-db, port 5432, database and username invoiceshelf and the generated DB_PASSWORD, then create the administrator account and company.
