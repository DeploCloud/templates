SickChill watches your TV shows, searches the configured indexers for new episodes and hands them to a download client such as a torrent or Usenet client. This template runs the official image with its configuration, database and cache on a persistent `/data` volume, plus persistent `/downloads` and `/tv` volumes for post-processing and the show library. The web interface is open without a login until you set credentials under Config > General > Interface. Indexers and a download client are configured from the web UI after deployment, and post-processing only works when the download client saves its files where SickChill can read them.
