Invidious is an open source alternative front-end to YouTube that lets you watch videos without ads or tracking. This template runs Invidious with its companion service, which fetches the video streams and is proxied through the main app, plus a PostgreSQL database whose tables are created automatically on first start. Accounts, subscriptions, playlists and preferences persist in the database volume, and the companion keeps its cache in its own volume. Account registration is open by default; anyone who can reach the instance can sign up.
