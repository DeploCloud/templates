Homelable draws your homelab as an interactive network diagram, discovers devices with nmap scans and checks their status live. This template runs the nginx web frontend and the FastAPI backend, which keeps its SQLite database, scan settings and uploaded floor plans on a persistent volume. Sign in as `admin` with the generated `ADMIN_PASSWORD`; the bcrypt hash the app expects is derived from it at every start, so changing the variable and redeploying changes the password. Optional features such as OIDC login, hypervisor and controller imports or the read-only live view are enabled by adding their variables to the `homelable-backend` service environment.
