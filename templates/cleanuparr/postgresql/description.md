Deploys Cleanuparr, a companion for Sonarr, Radarr and other arr apps that strikes and removes stalled, slow, failed or malicious downloads, together with a PostgreSQL 17 database for its data. The database persists in its own volume, while the config volume keeps the settings file, encryption keys and logs. Open the web UI after deployment to create the admin account, then add your arr apps and download clients with their URLs and API keys.
