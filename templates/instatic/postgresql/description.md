Deploys Instatic, a self-hosted CMS where the visual editor, content engine, media library and publisher run in one Bun server, with a bundled PostgreSQL database for teams with several authors editing at once. Content lives in the PostgreSQL volume, media, fonts, plugins and published pages persist in the uploads volume, and the runtime package cache sits in the data volume. Open /admin after deployment: the first visit creates the site and the owner account. Outgoing email and AI providers are configured later from the admin.
