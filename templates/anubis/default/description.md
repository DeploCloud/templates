Anubis is a reverse proxy that makes browsers solve a small proof-of-work challenge before reaching the site behind it, which keeps AI scrapers and aggressive crawlers out. This template follows the upstream compose example: Anubis protects a bundled NGINX static site whose files live on the persistent `anubis-site` volume. Replace those files with your site, or set `TARGET` to the internal URL of another service to protect it instead. Anubis itself keeps no state; a generated signing key keeps visitors' challenge cookies valid across restarts.
