HolyArrs is a single app that replaces a typical media automation stack: browse and request shows, movies and music, pick releases with your own rules, fetch subtitles and hand downloads to your downloader. Its databases, settings, backups, logs and the `holyarrs.key` master key persist in the `/config` volume; keep a copy of the key, since backups do not include it. Your library persists in the `/data` volume, prepared with `/data/downloads` and `/data/media/movies`, `/data/media/tv` and `/data/media/music`, writable by user 1000. Open the app right after deploying and create the owner account, then the setup asks for media folders (type the `/data/media` paths), a downloader and a search source. The downloader is not part of this template and must save its finished files where HolyArrs can read them under `/data`.
