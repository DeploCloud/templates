scanservjs is a web interface for SANE that lets you preview, scan, filter and OCR documents from network scanners and download the results. Finished scans persist on a named volume mounted at `/var/lib/scanservjs/output`. Add your scanners by setting `SANED_NET_HOSTS` (saned hosts), `AIRSCAN_DEVICES` (eSCL/WSD devices) or `PIXMA_HOSTS` (Canon bjnp) and redeploy. The bundled `config.local.js` enables HTTP basic auth from `SCANSERVJS_USER` and `SCANSERVJS_PASSWORD`; edit it for further customisation.
