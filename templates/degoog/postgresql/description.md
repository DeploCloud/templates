Degoog is a search aggregator that queries multiple search engines and shows the results in one page, extensible with engines, plugins, themes and transports from its store. This variant stores the indexer and favicon cache in an included PostgreSQL service, which handles concurrent writes and full-text search better than SQLite on a busy instance. Settings and installed extensions persist in the `/app/data` volume and the database in its own volume. The settings page is protected by the generated `DEGOOG_SETTINGS_PASSWORDS` value.
