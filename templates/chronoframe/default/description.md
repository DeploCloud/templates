ChronoFrame is a self-hosted photo gallery that extracts EXIF data, builds previews, plays Live Photos and shows shots on a map. This template runs the official image with its SQLite database, settings and auto-generated session secret kept on a persistent `/app/data` volume, and sets a per-instance secret for signing share-preview image URLs. On first visit a setup wizard creates the administrator and configures storage; choose local storage at `/app/data/storage` to keep photos on the same volume, or connect S3 or OpenList. A Mapbox or MapLibre token for the map view can be added later in the dashboard.
