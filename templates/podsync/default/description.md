Podsync turns YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud and Twitch channels, users or playlists into podcast feeds you can subscribe to from any podcast app. Episodes are downloaded with yt-dlp and served together with the RSS feeds at https://<domain>/<feed-id>.xml. Downloaded media lives on the data volume and feed metadata on a separate database volume. Before feeds fill up, edit the generated config.toml to add your YouTube or Vimeo API key and replace the sample feed with your own, then redeploy.
