Open Archiver archives, indexes and searches email from IMAP servers, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and imported PST, EML or mbox files. This template runs the app with PostgreSQL, Valkey, Meilisearch and Apache Tika for attachment text extraction. Archived emails are stored encrypted at rest on a persistent volume, next to persistent volumes for the database, queue and search index. On the first visit the `/setup` page creates the administrator account; ingestion sources are added from the dashboard afterwards.
