DumbAssets is a simple asset tracker for physical items, their components, warranties and routine maintenance. This template runs the official image and keeps the JSON data files, uploaded photos, receipts, manuals and notification settings on a persistent volume mounted at `/app/data`. There is no account system: set `DUMBASSETS_PIN` to a numeric PIN of 4 or more digits after deploying to keep it private. Set `APPRISE_URL` to receive warranty and maintenance notifications, and `CURRENCY_CODE` and `CURRENCY_LOCALE` to change price formatting.
