Deploys the official all-in-one Steedos image, which runs the Steedos low-code server together with an embedded MongoDB, Redis, Node-RED and an internal nginx under supervisord. The database, uploaded attachments, Node-RED flows, logs and the generated `docker.env` holding the internal database credentials persist on a volume mounted at `/steedos-storage`, and packages an administrator installs from the package manager persist on a second volume at `/app/.steedos`. Token and session signing secrets are generated at deploy time so sign-ins stay valid across restarts. After the first start, open the site and register: the first account creates the organization and becomes its administrator.
