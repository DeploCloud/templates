Shaarli is a minimalist, database-free bookmarking service for saving, tagging and sharing links. This template runs the official image (Nginx and PHP-FPM) and keeps the `data` directory, which holds the configuration, the bookmark datastore and its history, on a persistent volume, along with the thumbnail cache. On first visit Shaarli shows an installation form where you choose the login, password, title and timezone. Shaarli bans an IP after repeated failed logins and, behind the reverse proxy, every visitor shares the proxy's IP until its address is added under `security.trusted_proxies` in `data/config.json.php`.
