Deploys NodeCast TV, a browser-based IPTV player with Live TV, an EPG guide, movies and series from your Xtream Codes or M3U provider. The `/app/data` volume keeps the SQLite library, users, settings and the generated authentication secrets. The first visitor creates the admin account, then adds a provider under Settings - Content Sources and refreshes sources. Transcoding runs in software with FFmpeg; GPU acceleration is not configured.
