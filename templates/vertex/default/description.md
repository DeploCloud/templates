Runs the official Vertex image, an all-in-one manager for private tracker users that automates torrent seeding through RSS rules, download client control and series tracking. The `/vertex` volume keeps the configuration, the SQLite database, rules, logs and cached torrent files. After deployment, log in as `admin` with the random initial password from `/vertex/data/password` (run `cat /vertex/data/password` in the container terminal), then change it under global settings.
