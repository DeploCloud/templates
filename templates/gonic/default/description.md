Gonic is a lightweight music streaming server that implements the Subsonic API, so any Subsonic-compatible client can browse and play your library, with a small web panel for users, podcasts and internet radio. The SQLite database, music library, podcast episodes, playlists and transcode/cover cache each persist on their own named volume. Log in with admin / admin and change the password immediately, then copy your music into the music volume; new files are picked up by the filesystem watcher and a scan runs at every start.
