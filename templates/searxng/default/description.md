SearXNG is a metasearch engine that sends your queries to many search services and merges the results, without tracking or profiling users. This template runs the official SearXNG image plus a Valkey instance that is already connected for the optional bot limiter, which stays off as in the upstream defaults. The generated `settings.yml` in `/etc/searxng`, the cache in `/var/cache/searxng` and the Valkey data persist on named volumes. No account is needed; to customise engines or turn on the limiter, edit `settings.yml` in the config volume and redeploy.
