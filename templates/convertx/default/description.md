ConvertX is a web-based file converter that wraps FFmpeg, LibreOffice, ImageMagick, Pandoc, Calibre, Inkscape and other tools behind a single upload form. The SQLite database, uploaded files and conversion results live under `/app/data` on a persistent volume, and a cleanup that runs every 24 hours removes conversion jobs older than 24 hours together with their files. Open the site right after deployment to create the first account; public registration of further accounts is disabled by default.
