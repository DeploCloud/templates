This template deploys the Ente server (museum) on `api.<your domain>`, the Ente Photos web app on your domain and the public albums app on `albums.<your domain>`, backed by PostgreSQL and a Silo (MinIO-compatible) object store. Account data persists in the PostgreSQL volume and the end-to-end encrypted photos and videos persist in the object storage volume, which the apps reach through presigned URLs under `/b2-eu-cen` on your domain. Without an email server, sign-up verification codes appear in the `ente-museum` logs, and the first account created becomes the admin. Every account starts with a 10 GB storage limit, which the admin raises with the Ente CLI.
