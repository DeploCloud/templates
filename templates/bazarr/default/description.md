Deploys Bazarr, a companion to Sonarr and Radarr that finds and downloads subtitles for your series and movies. Settings and the SQLite database persist in the config volume, and subtitles are written next to the media in the movies and tv volumes. After the first start, enable authentication under Settings > General > Security, then add your Sonarr and Radarr connections with their API keys and set your subtitle languages and providers.
