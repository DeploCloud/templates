Medusa watches your TV shows, searches the configured indexers for new episodes and hands them to a download client such as a torrent or Usenet client. This template runs the official image with its configuration and database on a persistent `/config` volume, plus persistent `/downloads`, `/tv` and `/anime` volumes for post-processing and the show libraries. The web interface is open without a login until you set credentials in the general settings, and indexers and a download client are configured from the web UI after deployment.
