Lightdash is an open-source business intelligence tool that reads the models and metrics of a dbt project and lets your team explore them, build charts and share dashboards. This template runs the Lightdash server with a PostgreSQL (pgvector) database for its own app data, a RustFS S3-compatible store for exports and query results, and a headless Chromium for scheduled image deliveries. The database and object storage persist on named volumes. After deployment, register the first account (it becomes the admin), then connect your warehouse and dbt project; fill in the SMTP variables to enable invites, password resets and email deliveries.
