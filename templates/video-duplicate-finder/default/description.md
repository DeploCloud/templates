Video Duplicate Finder finds duplicate videos and images by visual similarity, including copies with a different resolution, frame rate or watermark. This template runs the official web UI image with FFmpeg included, protected by the generated VDF_WEB_PASSWORD. Settings live in one volume and the scan database, along with the optional AI matching components, in another. The videos to scan go in the `/media` volume, which the app can also delete duplicates from; fill it with your files, then add `/media` as a scan directory in the web UI.
