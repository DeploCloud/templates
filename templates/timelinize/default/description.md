Timelinize imports data exports from your accounts and devices (photos, videos, messages, location history, social media posts, contacts) and arranges them into one timeline you can browse by date, map, conversation or person. It runs behind a small basic auth gateway, since the app itself has no login. The timeline repository, with its SQLite database and the imported files, lives on the `/repo` volume, and the app settings, the list of opened timelines and the semantic search models and Python environments are kept on their own volumes. On first visit, create a timeline in `/repo`; data exports must be copied into the container before they can be imported.
