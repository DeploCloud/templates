Deploys AnonAddy (addy.io), an anonymous email forwarding service, as the upstream all-in-one container (Nginx, PHP-FPM and Postfix) with MariaDB and Redis. Application storage, the Postfix queue, DKIM and GPG keys persist in the data volume, alongside the database and Redis volumes. Aliases live under the deployment domain by default; change ANONADDY_DOMAIN and ANONADDY_HOSTNAME to use your own mail domain. Set RSPAMD_ENABLE to true to sign forwarded mail with DKIM and enable anonymous replies: the key and its DNS record are generated in /data/dkim on the next start. Receiving mail requires publishing SMTP port 25 and adding MX and SPF records for that domain.
