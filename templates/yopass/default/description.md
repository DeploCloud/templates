Yopass shares passwords, secrets and small files through links that expire or self-destruct after one view; the browser encrypts everything before upload and the decryption key stays in the link, never on the server. This variant runs the official Yopass server with a Memcached instance as its store, so nothing is written to disk and unread secrets are gone after a restart. No accounts or setup are needed: open the domain and create a secret.
