Deploys Matchering WEB, the official browser front end for the Matchering 2.0 audio mastering library, with its Django app, job queue and worker in a single container. Drop a target track and a reference track into the page and download the mastered target as 16-bit or 24-bit WAV, with an in-browser preview. The SQLite database and uploaded and processed audio live on a persistent volume at `/app/data`; sessions and files older than an hour are deleted when a new session starts. Upstream recommends at least 4 GB of RAM for processing.
