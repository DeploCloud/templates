DroppedNeedle is a music request and discovery app that searches MusicBrainz, fetches albums through a download client, verifies and files them in your library, and plays them in the browser or through Subsonic and Jellyfin apps. The encryption key in `/app/config`, the database and caches in `/app/cache`, installed plugins and pending drop imports each persist on their own volume, and the music library lives on a `/data` volume with a `/data/music` folder created for you. Create the first account on first visit to become the admin, then add `/data/music` under Settings > Library. This variant includes no download client: requests wait until a download source is set up, and new music arrives through drop imports or a download plugin.
