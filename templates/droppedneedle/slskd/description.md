DroppedNeedle is a music request and discovery app that searches MusicBrainz, fetches albums through a download client, verifies and files them in your library, and plays them in the browser or through Subsonic and Jellyfin apps. This variant adds an slskd Soulseek client that downloads into `/data/slskd/complete` on the same media volume as the `/data/music` library, so finished downloads are imported directly; the slskd data, DroppedNeedle's encryption key, database, plugins and pending imports all persist on volumes. Set your Soulseek account in SLSKD_SLSK_USERNAME and SLSKD_SLSK_PASSWORD, create the first account on first visit to become the admin, add `/data/music` under Settings > Library, and connect slskd under Settings > Download Client with URL `http://droppedneedle-slskd:5030` and the generated SLSKD_API_KEY.
