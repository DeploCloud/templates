FreeScout is a self-hosted help desk and shared mailbox that turns emails sent to your support addresses into conversations your team can assign and answer. This template runs FreeScout with its queue worker and scheduler in one container, backed by a MariaDB database. Configuration, the generated app key, attachments and installed modules persist under `/data`, logs under `/logs`, and the database on its own volume. The first start runs the migrations and creates the admin account from ADMIN_EMAIL and ADMIN_PASSWORD; afterwards connect each mailbox to your own IMAP and SMTP server in the mailbox settings.
