Deploys Aurral, a music discovery server that finds artists you like, downloads tracks through the built-in yt-dlp source or optional external clients, and plays them in the browser or any Subsonic app. The database, settings, users and yt-dlp staging files persist in the `/config` volume, and the music library persists in the `/data` volume at `/data/downloads/aurral`. A one-shot init container makes `/data` writable for the app user before it starts. Open the app after deployment to create the admin account and confirm the Library folder.
