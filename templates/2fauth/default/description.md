2FAuth is a web app to manage your two-factor authentication accounts and generate their security codes from any browser. This template runs the official image with a SQLite database; the database, uploaded icons and OAuth keys persist in the `/2fauth` volume. Open the app after deployment and register the first account, which becomes the administrator; registration stays open to anyone until you disable it in Admin > App Setup. Email features such as password reset are not configured and need SMTP settings to work.
