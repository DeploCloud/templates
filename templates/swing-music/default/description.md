Deploys the official Swing Music server, a web-based music player and streaming server for your own audio library. The `/config` volume keeps the database, settings and backups, and the `/music` volume holds the audio files the server scans. Music is not uploaded through the web UI, so copy your files into the music volume, then open the app: the first account created in the onboarding screen becomes the admin, and you choose the music folders to scan there.
