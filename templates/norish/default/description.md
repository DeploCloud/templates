Norish is a realtime recipe app for families and friends: import recipes from websites, videos and photos, plan meals in a shared calendar and keep grocery lists in sync. This template deploys the Norish server with PostgreSQL, Valkey for realtime events and jobs, and the Obscura headless browser that renders recipe pages for URL imports. Uploaded images, the database and the Valkey data persist on named volumes. The first account to sign in with email and password becomes the admin; AI providers, OIDC or OAuth sign-in and other options are configured afterwards under Settings > Admin.
