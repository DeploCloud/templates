Whisper ASR Webservice exposes OpenAI Whisper speech recognition as a REST API, running here on the CPU with the faster-whisper engine and the base model. Upload audio or video to /asr for transcription or translation to English as text, JSON, SRT, VTT or TSV, or to /detect-language; the root URL opens the Swagger UI to try both. Downloaded models are cached on a persistent volume so they survive redeploys. Change ASR_ENGINE (openai_whisper, faster_whisper, whisperx) or ASR_MODEL (tiny up to large-v3-turbo) in the environment to trade speed for accuracy.
