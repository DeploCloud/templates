Wallos is a personal subscription tracker that keeps your recurring expenses, categories, currencies and household members in one place, with payment reminders and spending statistics. This template runs the official image with nginx, PHP-FPM and the built-in cron jobs in a single container. The SQLite database and the uploaded subscription logos persist on named volumes. Register the first account right after deployment, since it becomes the administrator; currency conversion needs a free Fixer API key added in the settings.
